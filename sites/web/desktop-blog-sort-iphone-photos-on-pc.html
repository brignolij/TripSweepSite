How to sort your iPhone photos on a Windows PC

Jeffrey Brignoli · November 5, 2026

Using an Android phone? Read the Android guide: How to sort your Android photos on a Windows PC

An iPhone and a Windows computer? You can still sort your photos on a big screen instead of the phone. Here is what Windows can do on its own, and a faster method.

With the tools built into Windows

  • Photos app: imports a copy of your iPhone photos to the PC. Good for a backup, but it doesn't help you choose what to delete.
  • File Explorer: shows the iPhone's photos in folders such as 100APPLE, named IMG_0001 and so on, with no trips and no comparison of similar shots.

With PhotoCull, step by step

1. Install Apple Devices. Get the free Apple Devices app from the Microsoft Store: Windows needs it to read an iPhone.

2. Install PhotoCull. Get it from the Microsoft Store or download it for free (Windows 10/11, 64-bit). The download is not code-signed yet, so SmartScreen may warn on first launch.

3. Plug in the iPhone. Connect it with a USB cable, unlock it and tap Trust.

4. Sort trip by trip. The Trips view groups your camera roll by date and place and shows the space each trip takes. Start with the heaviest.

5. Compare, mark, delete. Similar photos carry a badge: compare up to six side by side, keep the best and mark the rest. The deletion is applied on the iPhone by the free TripSweep app through “Recently Deleted”, so you have 30 days to change your mind.

Good to know

  • Everything stays on your PC and your iPhone: no account, no cloud, no upload.
  • PhotoCull never modifies your originals and only deletes what you confirm.
  • Browsing and sorting are free, and sorting a folder of photos already on the PC is never limited.
