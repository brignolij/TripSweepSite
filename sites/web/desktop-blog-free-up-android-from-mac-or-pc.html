How to free up your Android phone's storage from a Mac or PC

Jeffrey Brignoli · November 10, 2026

Using an iPhone? Read the iPhone guide: How to free up your iPhone from a Mac or Windows PC

When an Android phone runs out of space, photos and videos are almost always the reason. Clearing them on a small screen is slow. From a computer you see more at once and decide faster, without buying more cloud storage.

Why a computer helps

A big screen shows dozens of photos at once, a mouse selects hundreds in seconds, and comparing near-identical shots side by side is far easier than swiping back and forth. A camera roll that would take an evening on the phone takes minutes.

The method

1. Connect the phone. Install PhotoCull on your Mac or PC and the free TripSweep app on the phone. Connect over Wi-Fi (Mac and Windows) or by USB (Windows), with TripSweep open on the Deletion tab.

2. Start with what weighs the most. The Trips view shows the space each trip takes; long videos and old vacations usually free the most.

3. Remove the near-duplicates. Bursts and repeated shots of the same scene carry a badge. Compare them side by side, keep the best one, mark the rest.

4. Clear screenshots and blurry shots. They rarely deserve to stay and are quick to mark as a batch.

5. Confirm on the phone. TripSweep applies the deletion and moves the photos to the phone's trash, where they stay 30 days. Empty the trash once you are sure, to get the space back.

Do I need Google One?

Cloud storage is a fine backup, but it keeps everything, duplicates included. Cleaning first means you need less storage, on the phone and in the cloud.

Good to know

  • No account and no upload: the phone and the computer talk over your own network or cable.
  • From the computer, free allows 6 deletions a day, Pro 15, and Pro + Desktop is unlimited.
