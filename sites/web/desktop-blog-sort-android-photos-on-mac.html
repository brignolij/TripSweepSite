How to sort your Android photos on a Mac

Jeffrey Brignoli · November 10, 2026

Using an iPhone? Read the iPhone guide: How to sort your iPhone photos on a Mac

Plug an Android phone into a Mac and, most of the time, nothing happens: macOS doesn't read Android phones over USB the way Windows does. Yet a Mac is the best place to sort a big camera roll, with a large screen, a mouse and side-by-side comparison. Here is what works.

What macOS can do on its own

  • Image Capture: may see the phone if you choose “Transfer photos” (PTP) in its USB notification, but only as a flat list of files, with no grouping and no way to compare similar shots.
  • Google's Android File Transfer: no longer maintained, and it only copies files anyway.
  • Cloud upload: sending everything to an online service just to sort it is slow, uses your data plan and puts your photos on someone else's server.

With PhotoCull, over Wi-Fi, step by step

1. Install both apps. Download PhotoCull for Mac for free (macOS 11 or later on Apple Silicon), and install the free TripSweep app from Google Play on the phone (Android 11 or later).

2. Same Wi-Fi. Put the phone on the same Wi-Fi network as the Mac and open the Deletion tab in TripSweep.

3. Connect once. In PhotoCull, click Android phone and pick your phone. Allow the Mac on the phone: the same 4-digit code shows on both screens. Next time, it reconnects by itself.

4. Sort trip by trip. The Trips view groups the camera roll by date and place, with the space each trip takes. Similar photos carry a badge: compare up to six side by side, keep the best, mark the rest.

5. Delete safely. Click Delete and confirm on the phone, in TripSweep. Photos go to the phone's trash for 30 days, so a mistake can be undone.

Good to know

  • The connection stays on your local network and is encrypted; nothing goes through the internet.
  • PhotoCull never modifies your originals and only deletes what you confirm on the phone.
  • From the computer, free allows 6 deletions a day, TripSweep Pro 15, and Pro + Desktop is unlimited. Browsing and sorting are always free.
  • Got an iPhone too? It connects to the Mac with a USB cable instead.
