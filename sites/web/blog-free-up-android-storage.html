How to free up Android storage without paying for Google One

Jeffrey Brignoli · November 10, 2026

Using an iPhone? Read the iPhone guide: How to free up iPhone storage without paying for iCloud

“Storage full” on an Android phone almost always means photos and videos. Settings → Storage shows it plainly, and most of that space is shots you will never look at again. Here is how to get it back without buying more cloud storage.

1. Start with what weighs the most

Long videos are the fastest win: a few minutes of 4K take as much room as hundreds of photos. Sort them by size, watch the first seconds and delete the ones you will never open again.

2. Delete similar photos, not just duplicates

Cleaner apps find exact copies, but the real waste is near-identical shots: bursts, five attempts at the same group photo, three selfies in a row. Keep the best of each group and delete the rest.

3. Clear screenshots and blurry photos

Screenshots of tickets, maps and conversations pile up unnoticed, and accidental or blurry shots too. They are quick to review as a batch.

4. Empty the trash

Deleted photos usually wait 30 days in the trash and keep taking space. Once you are happy with your choices, empty it to actually get the storage back.

5. Clean up old trips, one by one

Old vacations are the biggest block of photos you never look at. Going trip by trip is easier than scrolling thousands of photos: pick the heaviest trip, keep the highlights, delete the leftovers.

Do I need Google One?

Cloud storage is a fine backup, but it is not a cleaning tool: it stores everything, duplicates included. Cleaning first means you need less storage, wherever it is.
