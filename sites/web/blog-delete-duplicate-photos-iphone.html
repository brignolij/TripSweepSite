How to delete duplicate and similar photos on your iPhone

Jeffrey Brignoli · November 3, 2026

Using an Android phone? Read the Android guide: How to delete duplicate and similar photos on your Android phone

Duplicates quietly eat iPhone storage. Some are exact copies, but most are near-identical shots of the same moment. Here is how to find both and clean them up safely.

Duplicates and similar photos are not the same thing

An exact duplicate is the same file saved twice, for example after an import or a download. A similar photo is a different shot of the same scene: a burst, a few takes of a group photo, a selfie repeated until you were happy. Exact duplicates are easy to detect; similar photos are more common and take far more space.

The built-in way

Recent versions of iOS include a Duplicates album in the Photos app (under Utilities). It merges exact copies and is a good first pass. It does not group similar photos, so the bursts and near-identical shots stay in your library.

Removing similar photos

For similar shots you have to compare and choose. Open the group, look at sharpness, expressions and framing, keep the best photo and delete the others. Doing it by hand across thousands of photos is slow, which is why most people never finish.

Stay safe while you delete

  • Delete through the Photos app: deleted photos go to “Recently Deleted” for 30 days, so a mistake is reversible.
  • Keep favorites: mark the photos you love before cleaning.
  • Check iCloud Photos: if it is on, deleting on the iPhone deletes everywhere.
  • Empty “Recently Deleted” at the end to get the space back.
