How to delete duplicate and similar photos on your Android phone

Jeffrey Brignoli · November 10, 2026

Using an iPhone? Read the iPhone guide: How to delete duplicate and similar photos on your iPhone

Duplicates quietly eat an Android phone's storage. Some are exact copies, but most are near-identical shots of the same moment. Here is how to find both and clean them up safely.

Duplicates and similar photos are not the same thing

An exact duplicate is the same file saved twice, for example after a download, a messaging app or a restore. A similar photo is a different shot of the same scene: a burst, a few takes of a group photo, a selfie repeated until it looked right. File cleaners catch the first kind; the second kind takes far more space.

What Android offers

File manager apps can list exact duplicate files, and your gallery shows bursts. Neither compares near-identical shots for you or helps you pick the best one, so you end up flipping between photos one by one.

A faster way

1. Group by scene. TripSweep compares the content of your photos, not their names, and groups shots of the same scene, so a burst of ten becomes one decision.

2. Keep the best one. It suggests one shot per group; you can keep another one, keep several or skip the group.

3. Delete safely. Deleted photos go to the phone's trash for 30 days, so a wrong choice can be undone.

Good to know

  • Everything runs on the phone: no account, no upload. TripSweep is free on Google Play (Android 11 or later).
  • For a whole camera roll, comparing on a big screen with PhotoCull on a Mac or PC is faster still.
