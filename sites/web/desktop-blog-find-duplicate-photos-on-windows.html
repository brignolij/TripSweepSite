How to find duplicate and similar photos on Windows 10 or 11

Jeffrey Brignoli · November 10, 2026

Phone backups, camera cards and old copies leave the same photos in several folders on a PC. Some are exact duplicates, most are near-identical shots. Here is how to find both and clean them up safely.

What Windows offers

  • File Explorer: sorting by name, size or date helps with obvious copies, but it can't tell that two different files show the same scene.
  • The Photos app: good for viewing and importing, with no tool to find similar shots and pick the best.

With PhotoCull, step by step

1. Install PhotoCull. Get it for free for Windows 10 or 11 (64-bit), or from the Microsoft Store.

2. Open the folder. Click Open folder, or drop a folder onto the window. Subfolders and external drives are included.

3. Look at the badges. PhotoCull compares what the photos show, not their file names: exact copies, bursts and near-identical shots get the same “≈ N” badge.

4. Keep the best one. Compare up to six photos side by side, keep the sharpest, mark the rest.

5. Delete safely. Marked files go to the Recycle Bin, so you can restore them.

Good to know

  • JPEG, HEIC, PNG, TIFF, WebP and RAW files (CR2, CR3, NEF, ARW, RAF, ORF, RW2, DNG) are supported, as well as MOV and MP4 videos. HEIC thumbnails need Microsoft's free HEIF Image Extensions.
  • Photos are dated from their EXIF data, so copied folders still sort by the day they were taken.
  • Sorting a folder is free and never limited. Everything runs on your PC: no account, no upload.
